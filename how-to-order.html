How to order

One clear request. An agreed fabrication scope.

Start with a standard part number or a drawing-based requirement. Select complete assembly supply or fabrication-only, and confirm the details before production.

Start Your RFQ →

Send the request

Two supply routes

Be clear about who supplies the material.

01

Complete assemblies

Fluent supplies the agreed material and completes the fabrication under one order.

02

Fabrication only

03

Custom and repeat orders

Quote a one-off piece or repeat a drawing-controlled assembly with clear revisions and quantities.

For a standard configuration

  • Fluent part number and drawing reference
  • Required quantity and material requirements
  • Sourcing, finish, testing, and documentation
  • Project, ship-to location, and requested date

For a custom configuration

  • Dimensioned drawing or sketch for review
  • Material specification and the intended service
  • Connection geometry and face-to-face dimensions
  • Scope boundaries, quantity, inspection, and delivery
Drawings and purchase orders

After submitting your RFQ, reply to the confirmation email with attachments. Reference the agreed quote and approved drawing revision when issuing a purchase order. Delivery is confirmed during quotation.
