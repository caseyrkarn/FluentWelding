Before we quote

Send the requirements that define the assembly.

  • Liquid/service description and the project material specification
  • Operating conditions and specified corrosion or lining requirements
  • Connection schedule, geometry, supports, and fabrication drawings
  • Required inspection, sourcing documentation, quantities, and delivery location
Application requirements matter.

The project designer specifies the materials and any lining or coating system. A stainless grade alone does not establish suitability for every wastewater or leachate service.

Scope stays clear.

This is a component fabrication offering. Any tank supply, field installation, or system design scope must be separately agreed.
