Internal Funnels & Exterior 90s

Fluent Welding fabricates internal funnel and exterior 90 assemblies for tank applications. Released standard drawings are available in common sizes, with project-specific configurations reviewed from drawings and dimensions.

Request PricingView Standard Drawings
Two related product families

Specify the inside piece, the outside piece, or both.

The internal funnel and exterior 90 are separate order items. They are shown together on the released size drawings so buyers and fabricators can coordinate the complete tank penetration assembly.

Internal Funnel

Interior tank component used on the tank side of the connection and fabricated to the released dimensioned detail.

Exterior 90

Exterior elbow assembly with the released non-tank flange connection shown on the fabrication drawing.

Complete Coordination

Order either component separately or coordinate both pieces from the same released size drawing when the project requires the full arrangement.

Standard construction

Released details built from controlled drawings.

The published standard drawings govern dimensions. Project-specific changes are confirmed before fabrication release.

Released Material304 stainless steel on the released standard drawings. Alternate materials can be reviewed by quote and drawing confirmation.
PipeSchedule 10 stainless pipe is standard on the released IFN and E90 details.
Exterior ConnectionThe released exterior 90 details use a 150# raised-face flange on the non-tank connection.
Separate Order ItemsInternal funnels and exterior 90s have separate Fluent part numbers even when shown together on one size drawing.
Supply RouteComplete assembly supply or fabrication-only using buyer-furnished components can be reviewed during quoting.
Custom RequirementsAlternate materials, flange requirements, dimensions, finishes, and project-specific geometry can be reviewed from project drawings.
Released standards

Standard sizes & part numbers.

The released drawing family includes 4", 6", 8", 10", and 12" internal funnels and exterior 90s. Each size has a separate IFN and E90 part number.

IFN = Internal Funnel

Use the IFN part number when ordering the interior tank component from a released standard drawing.

E90 = Exterior 90

Use the E90 part number when ordering the exterior elbow assembly from the same released size detail.
