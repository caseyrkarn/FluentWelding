What can be included

Define the assembly, not just the size.

Overflow to grade

Flanged connection pieces, elbows, vertical spool sections, and offsets to the project discharge arrangement.

Inlet and suction/outlet connections

Spools, base elbows, and fabricated connection pieces coordinated with the tank and downstream piping.

Drains and accessories

Drain piping components, nozzles, and separately specified anti-vortex or overflow accessories in the agreed package.

RFQ requirements

What to send for pricing.

  • Fire tank layout and nozzle/piping schedule
  • Overflow routing, discharge elevation, and support details
  • Suction, inlet, and drain fabrication details
  • Material, flange, finish, code/listing, inspection, quantity, and delivery requirements
Project requirements govern.

A fabricated piping package is not a complete fire-protection system. Design, hydraulic requirements, listings, testing, and acceptance requirements remain project-specific and must be confirmed before release.
