Before we quote

Send the requirements that define the assembly.

  • Process/service description and applicable operating conditions
  • Piping layout, nozzle schedule, and approved connection details
  • Material, pipe schedule, flange facing, and finish requirements
  • Drawing revision, quantity, inspection needs, and requested delivery
Application requirements matter.

Custom process components are quoted against the specified duty and approved fabrication detail. No universal pressure, temperature, or chemical-service rating is implied.

Scope stays clear.

This is a component fabrication offering. Any tank supply, field installation, or system design scope must be separately agreed.
