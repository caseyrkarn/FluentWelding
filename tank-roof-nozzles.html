Roof nozzle fabrication

Standard details when they fit. Custom geometry when they don't.

Fluent Welding supports repeatable roof nozzle assemblies as well as project-specific roof penetrations. Released standards can be ordered by Fluent part number, while alternate sizes and configurations are reviewed against the project drawings before fabrication release.

Complete Assemblies

Fluent can supply the required material and complete the cutting, fitting, and welding under one order.

Fabrication Only

Buyer-furnished material can be fabricated when required by owner, sourcing, or project requirements.

Custom Roof Geometry

Standard construction

Designed around tank roof installation.

Released Fluent roof nozzle standards are based on dimensioned fabrication drawings. The approved drawing governs dimensions and configuration.

Released Standard Material 304 stainless steel on the currently released standard drawings. 316 stainless and alternate materials can be reviewed by quote and drawing confirmation.
Pipe Schedule 10 stainless pipe is standard on the currently released roof nozzle details.
Non-Tank Connection 150# raised-face slip-on flange is standard on the released details unless otherwise specified.
Roof Geometry Released drawings incorporate the applicable sloped-roof geometry shown on the fabrication detail.
Supply Route Available as complete assemblies or fabrication-only using buyer-furnished components.
Custom Materials & Finishes Alternate metals, coatings, finishes, projections, flange requirements, and project-specific geometry can be reviewed.
