Construction and custom requirements

Confirm the complete detail.

Published material

The supplied standard details use 304 stainless and Schedule 10 stainless pipe. Confirm any alternate grade, schedule, or material during quoting.

Connection requirements

The catalog specifies a 150# raised-face slip-on flange at the non-tank connection unless the approved quote or drawing states otherwise. Tank-side geometry follows the source sheet.

Project confirmation

Confirm roof slope, projection, flange geometry, dimensions, material sourcing, finish, and inspection requirements before fabrication release.

A published standard is not automatic approval for every tank.

Use the published part number only when the entire detail meets the project requirements. Custom geometry requires an approved fabrication drawing.
