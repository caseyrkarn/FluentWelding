Sidewall nozzle fabrication

Standard when possible. Custom when required.

Fluent Welding supports repeatable standard tank nozzle assemblies as well as project-specific configurations. Buyers can reference a released Fluent part number or submit drawings, dimensions, material requirements, or project specifications for review.

Complete Assemblies

Fluent can supply the material and complete the fabrication under one order.

Fabrication Only

Buyer-furnished material can be fabricated when required by the project or procurement requirements.

Custom Configurations

Nonstandard sizes, projections, flange requirements, materials, and configurations can be reviewed from project drawings.

Standard construction

Built around real tank installation requirements.

Released Fluent sidewall nozzle standards are based on controlled fabrication drawings. Project-specific requirements are confirmed during quoting and drawing review.

Released Standard Material 304 stainless steel on released standard drawings. 316 stainless and other materials may be reviewed by quote and drawing confirmation.
Pipe Schedule 10 stainless pipe is standard on the currently released sidewall nozzle details.
Non-Tank Connection 150# raised-face slip-on flange is standard unless the approved quote or fabrication drawing states otherwise.
Compliance Import, Domestic, AIS, and project-specific sourcing requirements can be identified during RFQ and PO release.
Supply Route Available as a complete assembly or fabrication-only using buyer-furnished material.
Custom Materials & Finishes Alternate metals, coatings, finishes, dimensions, and project requirements can be reviewed for custom fabrication.
